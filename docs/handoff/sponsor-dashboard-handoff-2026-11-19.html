Running it

The .env database URL (Neon) fails auth locally, so point at the local Postgres in Docker instead. The local DB hackwestern_spon is migrated through 0057.

DATABASE_URL="postgresql://postgres:postgres@localhost:5432/hackwestern_spon" \
SKIP_ENV_VALIDATION=1 npx next dev -p 3013

Mocks are plain HTML. From the worktree root:

python -m http.server 3040 --bind 127.0.0.1
# then open http://127.0.0.1:3040/mocks/

Decisions

Logged in docs/decisions.md on Nov 18. The short version:

  • Sponsors send preferences, organizers decide. Sponsors say how many reps, 1-on-1 or group or both, 15 or 30 min, when they're free, and who they want to meet (up to 40). Organizers run auto-fill over everyone, then assign and approve by hand.
  • Preferences live in the Google Sheet too, not only the database.
  • Rooms run Saturday 10 AM to 5 PM. Max 6 people per room, reps and hackers together. Each 1-on-1 rep gets their own room. That's on purpose.
  • Sponsors can't ask for more than their contracted chatting hours. Blocks are at least 1 hour. A warning shows past 3 hours with no break.
  • Shortlisted hackers are placed directly by organizers. Hackers never find out who shortlisted them. They just get an invite. Shortlist order doesn't matter.
  • Room clashes are resolved by hand. Organizers can message a sponsor back, like "rooms are tight at 10, can you start at 10:30".
  • Confirming the shortlist locks it, along with that sponsor's rooms and times. Once invites go out, a booking can't be dragged or deleted. Moving it takes Request change, which says how many hackers get notified.
  • Organizers can make their own events (owned by Organizer or assigned to a sponsor), book rooms, and add hackers. Auto-fill never double-books someone who's in an event.
  • QR scan reminder: when an organizer scans a hacker's QR code (scavenger hunt, food), pop up a reminder if that hacker has an unanswered coffee chat invite. Not built yet.
