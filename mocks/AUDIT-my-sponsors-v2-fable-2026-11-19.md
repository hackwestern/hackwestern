# Fable critique: My sponsors v2 sponsor table (2026-11-19)

## Why it doesn't work
1. It's a checklist, not a sponsor: 14 equal rows; 7 are fine automatic facts that bury the few that matter.
2. The Time column means three things (deadline, opening time, event); the "Next" highlight can point at something already over.
3. Labels only make sense in sequence ("Scheduled", "Invites", "Shortlist" vs "Judging shortlist").
4. Only one person is reachable (primary contact, no number); speakers appear only as subtext.
5. Breaks on phones: Status clipped, Action off-screen.
6. Organizer chores (speaking order, workshop setup) mixed with sponsor state; the sponsor's own help request isn't on its tab.

## Jazz's concerns
1. Missing contacts: agree (the screen's main job is "who do I call").
2. Action buttons are extra work: mostly agree. Only arrival, booth set up and coffee chat workshop attendance need a human; make them one tap on the value, no Action column.
3. Time shouldn't organize it: agree. Keep times only as values on real events; lateness goes to Needs attention.

## Redesign (tables, two columns: Attribute · Value)
- NEEDS ATTENTION (top, hidden when empty): open help requests, late items; the only buttons.
- PEOPLE: Name · Role · Phone · Text/Call for every rep (crown on primary), speakers, workshop presenters.
- LOGISTICS: Arrival ▾, Booth ▾, Portal link, CC workshop ▾, Their workshop (time · room).
- COFFEE CHATS: Preferences, Shortlist, Schedule (3 of 3 reps), Invites (14 of 18 confirmed).
- JUDGING: Shortlist, Presentations (5 · next 10:20, room), Top 5.
Automatic values are plain text, amber only when late. Human values are tap-to-advance pills. Lead chores stay in the Now table. All sponsors uses the same attributes as columns (one row per sponsor).
