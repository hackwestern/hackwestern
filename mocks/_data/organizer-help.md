# How the sponsor dashboard works (organizer Help content)

## Sponsors
- The sponsor list comes from the Spon Team sheet's **Sponsor Day-Of** tab. Status, booth, room and HW contact come from **Sponsor Status**.
- Statuses: Not arrived → Checked in → Booth set up. Changing a status here writes it to the sheet.
- **Digital-only sponsors** (Autodesk, Celsius, PCBWay, Shopify, uBit) aren't on site and get no portal link.
- **My sponsors** shows the sponsors you lead (your HW lead row on Sponsor Day-Of). When you're on call, you see everyone.

## Coffee chats
**What sponsors give us** (at the Friday 5 PM workshop): their team (one primary contact, every rep has a phone), hackers per chat (1–5; 1-on-1s can be 30 min), which 1-hour sessions each rep is free (10–11, 11–12, 1–2, 2–3, 3–4, 4–5), and about how many hours each rep should chat. The shortlist is optional, up to their number of hacker seats.

**Auto-schedule checks, for every placement:**
- Only sessions the rep ticked as free.
- No more sessions than the rep's hours (4+ = no cap).
- Never during the sponsor's own workshop, or 15 minutes before setup / after it ends (travel buffer).
- Never two chats in one room at once, never one rep in two places.
- Near rooms (2241–2243) first, then Ivey (1348–1358, a 15-minute walk).
- A sponsor's reps in the same session stay in one building.
- Sponsors with the fewest free sessions are placed first.
- Your manual moves are pinned: re-running Auto-schedule keeps them.
Anything that can't be placed becomes a **suggested fix** (another free session, Ivey together, or fewer hours). Apply it or contact the sponsor.

**Send (by 10 PM Friday):** saves the confirmed schedule, updates the locked **Coffee Chat Room Grid** tab, sends each sponsor's primary contact their schedule, and invites hackers in this order: people the sponsor scanned → their shortlist → hackers interested in that sponsor. Sponsors who didn't submit a shortlist by 9:30 PM get interested hackers.
**After Send:** an unanswered invite expires 20 minutes before the chat (35 for Ivey) and the seat goes to the next person automatically. Changing a sent chat asks those hackers to confirm again.

## Judging (Sunday)
- Only sponsors with a prize track judge (Sponsor Day-Of "Custom API / Prize Track").
- Round 1: score teams 1–10 (half points), shortlist 5–10, send. Presentations are scheduled from the shortlists. Round 2: rank 1st–5th from teams that presented.
- Results go to the **Sponsor Judging** tab with team IDs and Devpost links.
- **PCBWay** (digital): the spon team pastes their Devpost links; 4th and 5th place are optional. **TinyFish** judges remotely through its portal.

## Task pings
- Run-of-show tasks ping their owner by Slack DM; tasks without a clear owner go to the team channel. A failed ping shows "Text instead".
- Overdue tasks show first in "Needs you now" with Text and Mark done.
- **Sponsor requested help:** a sponsor taps Get help (message, name, call-back phone). Their HW lead gets a Slack DM (or whoever is on call, if the lead is off shift) with an "I'm on it" button. It shows at the top of the Now table with a timer. Tap I'm on it (Slack or here), then Resolved. If nobody takes it in 5 minutes, it's posted to the spon team channel. Your own sponsors' requests are highlighted; anyone can take any request.

## Where data lives
Spon Team RUN OF SHOW sheet: RUN OF SHOW, Sponsor Day-Of, Workshops, Opening + Closing Ceremonies, Coffee Chat Setup, Coffee Chat Room Grid (app-written, locked), Sponsor Status, HW Contacts, Sponsor Judging (app-written). Edits to grey tabs are overwritten by the app.
