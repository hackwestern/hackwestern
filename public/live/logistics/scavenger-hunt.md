To make the weekend even more fun (and to reward you for showing up, learning, and trying new things), we’re running a weekend-long Scavenger Hunt Points System. Earn points by attending workshops, joining activities, and winning mini-competitions, then redeem them for prizes at the help desk!

# 🔍 How It Works
- Attending a workshop or an activity earns points every time you go.
- Everything else (like winning a mini-competition) counts once.
- An organizer scans your Hacker badge card to give you points. Your total and every scan show up on the **Home** tab.

# 🏆 Prizes
Redeem your points for swag at the help desk, by the stairs outside the Grand Hall.

Points have no cash value and are only valid for Hack Western 13, so make sure you use them before the end of the event. Go explore, learn a ton, and snag some goodies along the way!
